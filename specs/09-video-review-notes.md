# Lesson video screening — 9 November 2026

The research screened 140 distinct candidate IDs across subject-specific searches. Public YouTube oEmbed metadata was available for 136. The selected catalogue contains 84 topic-resource associations using 78 unique videos across all 61 visible topics. A topic can share a resource with another topic. Individual stages may offer fewer resources when the available video is not a close enough match.

Selection favoured actual publisher uploads, concrete demonstrations, readable topic scope and an English-language fit. Rejected candidates included wrong-language lessons, unverified reuploads, a video titled as adverbs in search that publisher metadata identified as adjectives, reported speech for direct speech, prepositions for conjunctions, and algebraic multi-step equations for introductory word problems. Longer compilations were avoided when a focused lesson was available.

The catalogue retains publisher title, channel, source URL, relevance notes and review status. scripts/lesson-video-candidates.json records the search candidates; scripts/lesson-video-metadata.json records the public metadata checks. No video files are downloaded or rehosted.

## What has and has not been verified

This is a metadata and available-description screening, not a claim that every video has been watched or independently fact-checked throughout. Full human playback, pacing and accessibility review remains pending. Embedding and browser narration also depend on the viewing environment. Do not promote these resources as certified or universally best. The Video tab opens first; the local narrated example remains available in the Story tab and covers each concept/difficulty even when a matching video is missing. The UI labels broader resources as another view of the idea and never forces them to play.

## Release rubric

Before promoting a resource from screened to fully reviewed, watch its entire intended segment; check every mathematical or grammatical claim, current goal alignment, reading load, narration pace, distracting sales material, cultural/unit fit, subtitles and actual embed playback. Replace or narrow the stage mapping if any check fails. Keep one or two useful choices rather than filling a quota.
