# Parent learning insights

The default learner report is organized by subject, then practised topic, then evidence. Unpractised subjects show no evidence rather than zero ability. Keep the default view to a subject summary, one next-step spotlight, and collapsed topic details.

## Indicators

- Independent accuracy uses the latest 20 distinct question responses per topic, excluding hinted responses. Keep the actual numerator and denominator visible. Repeated facts and reversed addition/multiplication facts do not inflate the sample.
- A practised concept has steady evidence with at least eight distinct unhinted responses and at least 80% correct in its recent window. This describes practised steps, not the whole curriculum.
- Hints are a separate support signal. They are excluded from independent accuracy and error-pattern inference. Time taken does not lower understanding scores.
- Compare five earlier and five later distinct independent responses only when both windows are available. Changes of at least 20 percentage points are described with the actual before/after values.
- Recommend at most one primary next step per subject. Further topics and evidence stay collapsed.

## Error interpretation

A wrong final answer alone cannot establish the reason. Label patterns as possibilities and show supporting responses. Require at least two different unhinted wrong questions before surfacing a repeated numerical signature. Recognized signatures include missed carries, digit-wise subtraction without borrowing, answers matching another operation, decimal scaling, and adding fraction denominators.

A regrouping contrast needs at least four unhinted no-regrouping questions at >=80% correct, at least three regrouping questions below 60%, and matched operand digit widths and representations. Otherwise do not attribute the difference to regrouping. English error clusters use the particular concept and level goal. Unknown errors remain unclassified. An isolated error in an otherwise strong sample is explicitly not called a misconception or calculation slip.

New attempts optionally store the learning goal and whether the answer was typed or chosen. Older attempts remain usable through conservative prompt parsing and skill metadata. Reports use saved Level Up evidence and do not infer understanding from competitive game scores. Historical data is labelled with the last practice date.

Three fresh independent successes at the same goal retire its earlier error recommendations. Keep the older responses in the displayed sample; do not erase learning history. “Steady” is a topic snapshot across practised steps, never a claim that each concept or the complete topic is mastered.

## Validation

Test subject separation, empty and small samples, hint exclusion, repeated-question deduplication, matched-demand comparisons, supported patterns, ambiguous errors, isolated misses, trend thresholds, and legacy records. Preserve the classroom report separately.
